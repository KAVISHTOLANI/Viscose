Privacy Statement

How Viscose protects every detail shared with 9 Clothing shoppers and drop partners.

What We Hold

9 Clothing, operated under Viscose, only records the essentials required to deliver your pieces: name, contact, shipping city, product size preferences, and payment confirmations.

  • No biometric or government IDs are stored.
  • Marketing opt-ins are entirely optional.

Why We Use It

Information is applied strictly to fulfil orders, coordinate delivery partners, and share time-sensitive updates about current 9 Clothing drops.

  • We never resell or rent audience data.
  • Vendor access is limited to fulfilment status only.

How We Secure It

Records live on encrypted Viscose-managed devices with restricted internal access. Drop data is purged after fulfilment unless law requires a longer retention window.

  • You can request deletion once orders are delivered.
  • Access logs are reviewed after every launch.
Need clarifications? Email the Viscose data desk or tap the contact link in the footer and reference “Privacy — 9 Clothing” so our internal team can respond within two business days.
