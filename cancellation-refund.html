Cancellation & Refund

9 Clothing drops under Viscose are final sale. We do not host cancellations or refunds.

Why Final Sale

Pieces are produced per order with custom trims, so cancelling mid-way leaves fabric unusable. To keep drops sustainable we therefore keep every purchase locked.

No Return Window

We do not offer returns or exchanges for size swaps, preference changes, or gifting issues. Choose your measurements carefully before checkout.

Manufacturing Defects

If a parcel reaches you damaged, contact Viscose support within 24 hours with photos and your unboxing video. Valid factory issues will be repaired or remade—refunds are still not issued.

Placing an order confirms you understand that cancellations and refunds are unavailable for every 9 Clothing release powered by Viscose.
