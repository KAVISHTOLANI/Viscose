9 Clothing · Viscose

Shipping: 8–14 Days

Every 9 Clothing parcel leaves Viscose within eight to fourteen working days from payment confirmation.

Production Buffer

Each capsule is made-to-order. The first four days post purchase cover finishing, quality checks, and packing at the 9 Clothing studio.

  • Rush fulfilment is not available.
  • Public holidays extend the window automatically.

Dispatch & Tracking

Between day five and day eight we hand parcels to trusted national couriers. Tracking IDs are emailed and SMSed by the Viscose logistics desk.

  • Ensure your email and phone are accurate.
  • Tracking pages refresh every 12 hours.

Delivery Window

Couriers typically complete delivery by day fourteen for metros and tier-one cities. Remote pin codes may take the full timeline.

  • Missed attempts must be rescheduled within 24 hours.
  • Address changes are not possible after dispatch.
Your order is considered on-time as long as it arrives within the stated 8–14 business day window. For escalations, reach the contact page and reference your order ID.
