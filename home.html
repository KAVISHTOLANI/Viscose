About Viscose Sports

Viscose Sports is Hyderabad's fastest-growing, youth-led sporting movement founded with one mission — to redefine how our city plays, competes, and comes together through sport.

Created by young founders Kavish Tolani, Adesh Seetala and Akapeddi Pranav Kaushik, Viscose Sports represents a new generation of athletes, creators, fans, and leaders building something bigger than a league — a community.

In 2025, Viscose Sports is hosting large-scale tournaments in football, basketball, and eSports, with future expansions into cricket, F1 simulations, volleyball, badminton, MMA, racing leagues, and more. Every event is designed to be professionally run, high-energy, and unforgettable — giving players and fans a world-class experience.

Viscose is powered by collaboration. We proudly partner with Prime Hydration as our Official Hydration Partner and Decathlon as our Official Sports Gear Partner — strengthening the quality, scale, and professionalism of every tournament we host.

Today, Viscose Sports is not just a company — it's a youth movement. A platform built for passion, competition, opportunity, and community. A space where young athletes rise, teams are formed, champions are made, and Hyderabad comes alive with sport.

At Viscose, sports aren't just what we play — they're who we are.
